A radial menu for SolidWorks.

RadialDeck gives SolidWorks a ring of its own: hold the hotkey while SolidWorks is in front, flick toward a slice and let go. The gallery has 8 ready-made actions for it, among them zoom fit, rebuild, force rebuild. Everywhere else your usual ring stays as it is.

How to add them

Open RadialDeck Settings, go to the gallery, search for “SolidWorks” and add the actions you want. If SolidWorks is installed when you first start RadialDeck, the Welcome window can suggest them for you.

A ring that appears only in SolidWorks is part of Pro, with a 14-day trial built in. In Free you can put the same actions on one of your two rings.

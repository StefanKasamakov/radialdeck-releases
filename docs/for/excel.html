A radial menu for Excel.

RadialDeck gives Excel a ring of its own: hold the hotkey while Excel is in front, flick toward a slice and let go. The gallery has 29 ready-made actions for it, among them paste values only, freeze panes, toggle filter. Everywhere else your usual ring stays as it is.

How to add them

Open RadialDeck Settings, go to the gallery, search for “Excel” and add the actions you want. If Excel is installed when you first start RadialDeck, the Welcome window can suggest them for you.

A ring that appears only in Excel is part of Pro, with a 14-day trial built in. In Free you can put the same actions on one of your two rings.

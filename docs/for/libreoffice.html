A radial menu for LibreOffice.

RadialDeck gives LibreOffice a ring of its own: hold the hotkey while LibreOffice is in front, flick toward a slice and let go. The gallery has 15 ready-made actions for it, among them export as PDF, libreOffice Writer: Find/Replace, libreOffice Writer: Comment. Everywhere else your usual ring stays as it is.

How to add them

Open RadialDeck Settings, go to the gallery, search for “LibreOffice” and add the actions you want. If LibreOffice is installed when you first start RadialDeck, the Welcome window can suggest them for you.

A ring that appears only in LibreOffice is part of Pro, with a 14-day trial built in. In Free you can put the same actions on one of your two rings.

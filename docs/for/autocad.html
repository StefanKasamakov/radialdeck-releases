A radial menu for AutoCAD.

RadialDeck gives AutoCAD a ring of its own: hold the hotkey while AutoCAD is in front, flick toward a slice and let go. The gallery has 29 ready-made actions for it, among them zoom extents, purge, plot. Everywhere else your usual ring stays as it is.

How to add them

Open RadialDeck Settings, go to the gallery, search for “AutoCAD” and add the actions you want. If AutoCAD is installed when you first start RadialDeck, the Welcome window can suggest them for you.

A ring that appears only in AutoCAD is part of Pro, with a 14-day trial built in. In Free you can put the same actions on one of your two rings.

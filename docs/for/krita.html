A radial menu for Krita.

RadialDeck gives Krita a ring of its own: hold the hotkey while Krita is in front, flick toward a slice and let go. The gallery has 9 ready-made actions for it, among them new layer, duplicate, merge down. Everywhere else your usual ring stays as it is.

How to add them

Open RadialDeck Settings, go to the gallery, search for “Krita” and add the actions you want. If Krita is installed when you first start RadialDeck, the Welcome window can suggest them for you.

A ring that appears only in Krita is part of Pro, with a 14-day trial built in. In Free you can put the same actions on one of your two rings.

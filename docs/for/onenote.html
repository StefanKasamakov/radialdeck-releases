A radial menu for OneNote.

RadialDeck gives OneNote a ring of its own: hold the hotkey while OneNote is in front, flick toward a slice and let go. The gallery has 8 ready-made actions for it, among them new page, new section, search. Everywhere else your usual ring stays as it is.

Download free Get Pro · 24 € a year

8 actions, ready to add

  • New pageAdd a new page in current section
  • New sectionCreate a new section
  • SearchSearch all notebooks
  • To-do tagApply / clear To Do tag
  • ImportantApply / clear Important tag
  • LinkInsert a hyperlink
  • Email pageSend current page by email
  • DockDock OneNote to desktop

How to add them

Open RadialDeck Settings, go to the gallery, search for “OneNote” and add the actions you want. If OneNote is installed when you first start RadialDeck, the Welcome window can suggest them for you.

A ring that appears only in OneNote is part of Pro, with a 14-day trial built in. In Free you can put the same actions on one of your two rings.

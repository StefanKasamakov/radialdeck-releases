The gesture

Every slice is one short flick away, always in the same direction. After a few days you stop looking.

Hold, flick, release Free

Hold the hotkey, move toward a slice, let go. Ctrl+Shift+Space or any chord you like. A slice can hold another ring, which opens right under the cursor, so a choice three levels deep is still one stroke.

Rings inside rings Pro

Arrow keys point like a joystick Free

Up is the top slice, right the right one, two arrows together pick the diagonal, Enter runs it. Your hands never leave the keyboard.

Type to find Free

With the ring open, type the first letters of a label and that slice lights up. Any keyboard layout, Cyrillic included, and the letters never reach the app underneath.

Nested rings as bubbles Pro New in 0.15

Rest on a slice that holds a ring and its slices fly out around it as bubbles; a bubble can hold a second layer. Try it live.

How the ring opens Pro

Fade, Pop, Bloom, Spin or Ripple instead of Instant, with a Preview button in Appearance.

Mouse buttons Free

Side buttons, the middle button, or hold the right one. A quick right click is still a right click.

Stay open Free

Shift+click runs a slice and keeps the ring open for the next one.

Quiet in full screen Free

Games, films and slideshows get their keys back until you leave full screen.

Knows where you are

Before it draws anything, RadialDeck looks at the window in front: which program, which files are selected, what text is highlighted.

A ring for each program Pro

Outlook gets Reply and your templates, Explorer gets Extract and Terminal here, Excel gets Sum and Freeze panes. The gesture stays the same; the ring follows the program. It matches the process name or the window title.

Acts on the files you selected Free

Select files in Explorer or on the desktop and flick: extract, zip, hash, convert, a terminal in that folder. Commands get the selection as {file}, {dir}, {name}.

RadialDeck Excel is open. Click to add a ready-made Excel ring with 8 shortcuts.

Ready-made rings, offered once Pro

Open the ring over a program the gallery knows, from Excel and Teams to VLC and Blender, and RadialDeck offers a ring built for it. One click and it is yours.

The highlighted text Free

{selection} is what you highlighted: search it, translate it, send it to a script.

Switch, do not reopen Free

If the program is already open, the slice brings it to the front instead.

What a slice does

A slice is an action, and any action can sit in any ring.

A ring of your open windows Free

Drop ring

Drag a file, tap Shift, drop it on a slice. The new file lands next to the original, which is never changed.

What the file can become Pro

Tap Shift while you drag. Pictures: smaller, JPG, PNG, one PDF, the text in them, a copy without location. Video: smaller, MP4, MP3, GIF. PDF pages as pictures, or smaller. Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF when Office is installed.

Video and audio use FFmpeg, PDFs Ghostscript, if you have them.

What to do with it Pro

Tap Ctrl instead: ZIP, Copy to, Move to, Share, the path, SHA-256, and your own drop actions.

Convert the selected files Pro

The same ring from your hotkey, for the files selected in Explorer.

A normal drop stays normal Free

Drop anywhere else and the file moves or copies as it always did.

Clipboard and text

Copy, flick, paste. The transforms are built in, so nothing starts up behind them and they work offline.

32 text transforms Free

Plain text, UPPER case, links without tracking, pretty JSON, sorted and de-duplicated lines, every e-mail address in a text, Base64, Latin to Cyrillic and back, word counts.

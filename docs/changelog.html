0.11.0

2026-10-02

Added

  • A three-step tour after the first run. The card that said "try it now" now teaches the three ways to pick a slice, one at a time, and moves on by itself when you do each: hold the hotkey and flick, then tap it and click, then type the first letters and press Enter. The last card says where the ready-made rings and actions are and opens Settings. ✕ skips the rest.
  • Tips for the first ten openings: one short line under the ring each time, each about something else it can do (tap instead of hold, type to find, arrow and number keys, Shift+click, nested rings, the wheel over a volume slice, rings per program, the gallery). After ten they stop. They never take focus and every click goes through them. The count is kept in the registry, not in config.json.

0.10.13

2026-10-01

Fixed

  • Settings opened too small for what it holds: at the default size Save and Close were cut off the bottom bar, and the Add / Remove / move buttons sat below the ring preview, out of sight. The window now opens as big as its content needs, as far as the screen allows, also when a smaller size was saved before, and grows when a slice's form gets longer (a macro, a command). The bottom bar keeps only how the ring looks, the gear, and Save / Close; Import, Export, the licence and the themes folder moved into the gear.
  • Labels in a ring no longer look crooked. Each label used to find its own distance from the hub and its own size, so a slice and its mirror image sat at different heights. A ring now shares one distance and one size; only a label that cannot fit at all finds its own place.
  • A word too long for its slice even at the smallest size is shortened with an ellipsis ("TRANSLITER…") instead of being split over two lines.
  • Icons that all but vanished in some themes (Wasteland: dark green on dark green) are readable: an icon colour too close to what it is drawn on falls back to the label colour.

0.10.12

2026-10-01

Added

  • Flick without holding the hotkey. After a quick tap the ring waits for a click, and now a flick works there too: rest on a slice with a nested ring and it opens, leave the ring quickly through a slice and it runs. Leaving slowly does nothing, so moving the mouse away is still safe. This also covers letting go of the hotkey a moment before a nested ring opened, which used to leave that ring waiting for a click. On by default; Settings > gear > "Flick without holding the hotkey" turns it off. debug.log now says why a ring waited for a click.
  • A photo can come off again: next to Photo… a ✕ appears when the style has one. A style that Photo… made goes away and the style it came from returns; any other style keeps its colours without the picture.

Changed

  • The Style list shows every style as a small ring with its own icons, in a grid, instead of a long list of names that opened scrolled to the middle.

0.10.11

2026-09-30

Added

  • A photo behind the ring, the way a phone keyboard takes one. Settings > Photo… picks a picture; it is scaled down, blurred a little and darkened so the labels stay readable, and the slices turn see-through over it. The result is a theme of its own, the current style plus the picture, carried inside one file so it can be shared like any other. Pictures in a theme can only be embedded data or a file in the themes folder, never a path elsewhere or on another machine.
  • Icon packs, the way a phone restyles every icon at once, and each theme brings the pack that suits it: in the Minecraft theme every icon becomes pixel art in an inventory slot, Kanto gets 8-bit buttons, Plumber the Mushroom Kingdom colours, Cyberpunk and the grid themes glowing hexagons, Neon and Synthwave glowing rings, Aero and Frost glossy bubbles, Sakura and Paper stickers, Gold and Arrakis gems. "Icons like the theme" is the default and changes with the theme; any pack can also be picked on its own next to the ring style - fourteen in all, among them Tiles, Bubbles, Mono and Outline. A pack works on whatever icon a slice already has - a Windows symbol, an emoji, a program's icon, your own picture - so nothing has to be redrawn. Your own packs are small *.json files in the iconpacks folder.
  • A way back that works while the trigger is still held. Inside a nested ring the hub shows a back arrow; move the pointer out, then rest it in the centre for a quarter of a second and the ring goes up a level. With the right button as the trigger (hold right click) this was missing altogether: going back was a right click, which is the button already in your hand. In click mode a click in the centre goes back, as before, and now it shows.
  • Open another ring: a slice can open any ring from your config right where you are, like a nested ring. Keep the text tools in one ring and reach it from the Excel ring, the Outlook ring and the main one, instead of copying the same slices into each. Pick "Open another ring" as the action and choose the ring by name; it counts as a nested ring, so it is Pro, and it cannot sit on the third level, because it opens a level of its own.
  • Double-tap the hotkey to keep the ring open. Press it twice quickly and the ring stays on screen for clicks, number keys, arrows and the wheel, however long the first press took; before, the second press closed it. Off by default (Settings › gear); the window is 300 ms, and "doubleTapMs" in config.json takes 150 to 800.
  • Keep the ring open while Ctrl, Shift or Alt is held, the way Alt+Tab works: hold Ctrl+Shift, tap Space, flick, and let go of Ctrl+Shift to run the slice, or let go on nothing to close. There is no rush to flick before Space comes up. Off by default (Settings › gear), because letting go of Space is what runs a slice today.
  • My icons: your own pictures as slice icons. Pick one with Image file or drop PNG and ICO files on the icon window; they are copied into the icons folder (%APPDATA%\RadialDeck\icons), so a ring keeps its icon when the original file moves, and the whole folder can be copied to another PC. A new "My icons" tab lists them and a button opens the folder.

Fixed

  • Long labels stay inside their slice. Each label - its icon and every line of text - is now checked against the real shape of the wedge, which is narrow near the hub and tall only at the sides, and gets the largest size, line width and distance from the hub at which all of it fits, on up to three lines. Before, a long word was allowed over the edge into the next slice ("TRANSLITERATE" in the Neon theme) and many labels were cut short ("Delete duplica…", "Isolate the sel…"). Only a label no slice could hold at a readable size is still shortened, and then inside its own slice.

0.10.9

2026-09-28

Security

  • An update is checked before it is run. The installer's SHA-256 is published with every release, and it is now compared before anything is started; a file that does not match is refused instead of installed. The download also gets a name nobody can guess, because a fixed path in the temp folder can be created in advance by anything else running as you, and what lands there is executed. This catches a broken download and a swapped file. A release tampered with at the source would carry its own hash, which is what signing the installer is for.

Changed

  • The 14-day trial no longer starts over when the licence file is deleted: the start is kept in the registry as well, and the earlier of the two dates counts.

Fixed

  • Where several people are signed in to one machine at the same time, a Remote Desktop host in particular, only the first session could use --settings, --menu, --reload and ring links; the rest logged an error twice a second and answered nothing. Each session now has a channel of its own, and the command line is unchanged.
  • The first-run list showed "in explorer" next to "in EXCEL". The program a ring is tied to reads in lower case throughout.

0.10.8

2026-09-28

Added

  • The first run offers rings for the programs you actually have. RadialDeck reads the Start Menu, matches it against the gallery and adds up to six ready-made packs - Excel, Word, VLC, Edge, Acrobat, whatever is there - left unticked, so nothing is created without being asked for.
  • Thirteen recipes: the path and name of the selected file in the shapes people actually need (quoted for a terminal, name only, forward slashes for WSL), three renames by rule, decimal to hex and back, Unix time to a readable date, a fresh GUID, and typing the clipboard out one character at a time for RDP sign-in screens, virtual machine consoles and BIOS, where pasting is refused on purpose.

Security

  • A recipe no longer reads a file name as code. A file may legally be called $(...), and PowerShell evaluates that inside a double-quoted string, so a recipe written as (Get-FileHash "{file}").Hash ran whatever the person who named the file chose; a downloaded archive was enough to deliver it. Twenty recipes were affected, and they now receive the selection through the environment, where it is data and not code.
  • A ring that arrives from a link can no longer reach off this computer while it is only being previewed. An icon path pointing at another machine handed that machine your Windows credentials, before anything had been saved.
  • The warning before such a ring is saved says what each slice runs instead of the label someone chose for it. A slice called "Open Downloads" holding a download-and-run command used to read as harmless.

Changed

  • Pro is now 24 € a year, VAT included (was 19 €). Keys already bought keep working as before.
  • Copy text from the screen has its own selection instead of the Windows snipping overlay: the screen freezes, you drag over the text, Esc or a right-click cancels. No Snipping Tool notification, no picture left on the clipboard or in clipboard history, and it works where a company policy removes the Snipping Tool.

Fixed

  • Type to find works with a Cyrillic keyboard layout: with Bulgarian or Russian active, the C key types "ц", and English labels were never found. The keys now also count as Latin letters. Typing also works while the hotkey is still held.
  • Copy text from the screen reads Cyrillic and English together: with English first in the language list, Windows' English recognizer turned Bulgarian text into "TbpceTe necHM". A Cyrillic recognizer the user has now leads, a snip with almost no Cyrillic still goes to the English one, and an English word it spells in Cyrillic ("Неуу" for "Heyy", "З рт" for "3 pm") takes the English reading of the same spot. The same holds for every other alphabet: a Greek, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, Korean or Arabic recognizer leads over English (Greek words get the same repair), within Latin the user's own language beats English (Polish keeps ą and ł), and when Windows has no recognizer installed for one of the user's languages, the first snip says so and where to add it.
  • Opening a shared ring link brings RadialDeck's Settings to the front instead of behind the browser, and the link itself no longer lands in the log.
  • Re-opening the first-run screen from Settings replaced everything: all your rings, the theme, the language, the ring size and the update settings. It adds the rings you pick and leaves the rest alone.
  • Twenty-three recipes duplicated another one for the same program, so a ring offered for that program showed the same thing twice and wasted a slice: VS Code offered "Palette" twice, Outlook repeated seven of its eight.
  • The Developer pack pointed at a recipe that does not exist and made a ring of five slices instead of six.

0.10.6

2026-09-23

Added

  • RadialDeck steps aside while a full-screen window is in front (a game, a video, F11, a slideshow): hotkeys are released and hooks removed, which is also what anti-cheat software looks for, and everything comes back when you leave full screen. A maximised window does not count. On by default; the switch is in Settings > Preferences.
  • Mouse clicks in macros: click left, click right, click middle or click double, optionally at screen coordinates (click left 640,400); {"click": "left 640,400"} in config.json.
  • Type to find: with the ring open, typing letters highlights the first slice whose label starts with them (then one with a word starting with them, then one containing them); Enter picks it, Backspace deletes a letter. Works in any keyboard layout, Cyrillic included. Typed letters never reach the app underneath.
  • Copy text from the screen ("type": "ocr"): the slice opens the Windows snipping overlay, you mark an area, and the text in it lands on the clipboard. Uses the OCR built into Windows in the languages you have installed; nothing leaves the PC. Also in the gallery as "Copy text from the screen (OCR)".
  • Scroll to adjust ("type": "adjust"): with the ring open, point at the slice and turn the mouse wheel to change the volume, zoom, browser tab or track; the ring stays open while you scroll, and a click mutes, resets the zoom, opens a tab or plays/pauses. Four ready-made slices in the gallery.
  • Ready-made rings for known programs: open the ring over a program the gallery knows (Excel, Teams, VLC, Blender and about 90 more) that has no ring of its own, and RadialDeck offers, once per program, to add one built from its recipes. Click the notification to add it.
  • Share a ring as a link: the new Share button next to a ring copies https://radialdeck.com/r#…, a page that shows what is in the ring and opens it in RadialDeck. The ring travels inside the link after the '#', which never reaches a server. RadialDeck adds a shared ring to Settings unsaved, so you look it over and press Save yourself, and warns when it would run commands or programs.
  • Microsoft Store package: build\msix.ps1 builds dist\RadialDeck-<version>.msix (the Store signs it, no certificate needed). In the Store build, Start with Windows uses the package's startup task (Task Manager, Startup apps), updates come from the Store (no update checks, menu item or settings), radialdeck:// links come from the manifest, and "Open folder" buttons find the files where Windows keeps them for packaged apps. The release workflow builds it when the Partner Center identity is set as repository variables.

Changed

  • Settings and the other windows get their own glass look instead of the Windows 11 Mica backdrop: graphite (or milky ceramic in light mode) with soft blue and teal light, frosted panels and a title bar in the same colour. Mica took its tint from the wallpaper (purple and pink on some desktops) and went grey whenever the window lost focus; the new look is the same focused or not. "Solid windows" still gives a flat background.
  • Every action type has an icon in the action list and next to the chosen action.
  • radialdeck.com's Download buttons fetch the installer directly instead of opening the GitHub release page. Every release also carries a copy named RadialDeckSetup.exe, the fixed name behind …/releases/latest/download/RadialDeckSetup.exe.

Fixed

  • "Switch to it if it is already open" no longer crowds the Label field below it.
  • Saving in Settings no longer forgets settings it does not show, such as a skipped update version.

0.10.3

2026-09-23

Added

  • "Help" and "Report a problem…" in the tray menu. The report opens a prefilled e-mail with the version and Windows build and selects debug.log so it can be attached; nothing is sent unless you send the mail.
  • Before a config file is migrated to a newer format, the original is kept as config.v<N>.backup.json.
  • {selection}: the text selected in the app you are in, copied only when a slice that uses it runs, so there is no need to press Ctrl+C first. Commands and transforms can take it as input ("input": "selection", or "Selected text" in Settings). Terminals are copied with Ctrl+Shift+C, and with nothing selected the clipboard is left untouched.
  • "Switch to it if it is already open" for app slices ("focus": true): brings the open window forward instead of starting another copy, and using it again cycles through that app's windows.
  • Open windows ring ("type": "windows"): a slice that opens a ring of your open windows, front-most first, with their app icons; flick at one to switch to it. Titles are shown on the ring but never written to the log.
  • "Keep the window on top" ("type": "topmost") toggles always-on-top for the window you are in; Windows has no built-in way to do this. The gallery gains it together with move-to-other-monitor, maximize, minimize, snap left/right and the open windows ring.
  • Shift+click (or Shift+1..8) in click mode runs the slice and keeps the ring open, for pressing Vol+ a few times or launching several things in a row.
  • Hold the right mouse button as a trigger ("RightHold", or right-click the hotkey box in Settings). A quick right click is replayed as a normal one so context menus still open, and a right-drag is let through as soon as the mouse moves; only a press held for a quarter of a second opens the ring. The right button is watched only while some ring uses it.
  • Terms of use and a refund policy (30 days, no questions asked) on the website; the privacy policy now spells out what is kept from a purchase, why, for how long, and how to have it deleted or complain.
  • Arrow keys pick slices: right/down walk clockwise, left/up the other way, Enter selects, Backspace goes back a level. Empty positions are skipped, and moving the mouse hands the highlight back to the cursor.

Changed

  • The ring and windows are drawn in software instead of through Direct3D. On an Intel Arc laptop that cut the app's private memory from 129 MB to 57 MB idle (193 MB to 89 MB with Settings open) with no visible difference. RADIALDECK_GPU=1 switches Direct3D back on.
  • The Pro preset only uses what a stock Windows PC has: zip and unzip use Windows' own archive commands instead of 7-Zip, and the Claude Code, Ghostscript, pandoc, ffmpeg, Notepad++, Everything, Git, Cyrillic and Obsidian slices are gone. "Translate BG" is now "Translate".
  • Free now runs two rings (not tied to an app) and every action type except macros / Repeat and the clipboard history ring: transforms, clipboard input and output for commands, Explorer placeholders and {selection} are free. Pro (29 €, one-time) adds unlimited rings, app rings, nested rings, macros, the clipboard history ring and command-line control. The site, README and licence window now say exactly this.

Fixed

  • Errors on background threads and in unobserved tasks are now written to debug.log instead of vanishing.

Removed

  • The two gallery recipes that opened Claude Code in a fixed %USERPROFILE%\Projects folder, and Claude Code from the Developer starter pack.

0.10.0

2026-09-21

Fixed

  • Settings dropped every pin on Save: the editor and the preview were right, the file was not.
  • Picking a language other than English or Bulgarian did not stick: the config validator still only accepted those two and quietly reset everything else to "system". It now accepts every language that ships.
  • A machine started against a central config (--config) stayed on it: the autostart entry and the silent self-update now carry the path through, instead of quietly handing the app back the local config after an update or a sign-in.

Added

  • A theme studio on the website: build a ring theme in the browser with a live preview that is the same renderer the application uses, then download the file or open a pull request against the community themes repository with one button. The gallery page shows all 25 built-in themes plus whatever the community has sent in.
  • A clipboard history ring (Pro). Add a "Clipboard history ring" slice and flicking at it opens a ring of the last things you copied; flick at one to paste it back, exactly as it was. Nothing about it is stored: the entries live in memory only, never reach config.json or the log, and are forgotten when RadialDeck closes. Windows is only asked about the clipboard at all once a ring actually uses the feature, so it stays off for everyone else.
  • Slices can be pinned to a position. A pinned slice keeps its place on the ring when you add or remove others, so the flick you have learned keeps working; unpinned slices fill whatever is left and a position nobody claims stays empty rather than pulling its neighbours around. Tick "Keep this slice at position N" in Settings, drag a slice onto an empty position, or set "pin": 1..8 in config.json.

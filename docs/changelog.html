RadialDeck 0.15.0

2026-10-22

Added

  • Nested rings as bubbles (Pro), in Appearance: point at a slice that opens a ring and its slices float out around the ring as bubbles; let go on one to run it. A bubble that holds a ring of its own fans it out as a second layer.
  • The ring opens with a spring, right where the pointer is (Pro; the default for new settings). The lit slice is a soft light that glides from slice to slice, in each theme's own highlight colours.
  • See-through glass, in Appearance › Window look: a real Windows 11 Acrylic look for RadialDeck's windows (Windows 11 22H2 or later).
  • What's new in the Settings sidebar: every release, with short clips that play like reels and a button to try each thing. The window after an update shows the same clips. The clips come with the app; nothing is downloaded.
  • AI assistants in the gallery: ask Claude, ChatGPT or Grok about the selected text, search it in Perplexity, summarise the clipboard, open Copilot, Gemini, DeepSeek or Le Chat; rings for the Claude and ChatGPT apps.
  • Programs that are not on this PC show their own logo: 37 more programs have one (sources in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
  • Icon packs show sample icons in the ring style, in the row and in the list.

Changed

  • "For your apps" finds far more of your programs: Microsoft Store apps, installs for one user, new versions in a new folder. A Scan again button looks again.
  • Drop ring: the file sits small in the hub, picks a slice by direction and moves on springs. Windows' big drag picture no longer covers the ring.
  • "What's new" in the tray menu opens Settings › What's new.

Fixed

  • After sleep the ring could open without its animation until RadialDeck was restarted; Windows' animation setting is now read each time.
  • Smoother on a laptop at 150% scaling: no crash when moving between monitors, no blur.

RadialDeck 0.14.2

2026-10-20

Added

  • "What's new" shows the new way the drop ring opens and the Ctrl ring, with a moving demo of each, to everyone who updates.

Changed

  • The drop ring opens when Shift is tapped mid-drag (pressed and let go), not as soon as it goes down. Holding Shift until the drop is how Explorer moves a file, and the ring opening under the pointer used to catch that drop. While the key is down, a keycap by the pointer shows that letting go opens the ring.
  • Two rings, as in Tangerine: Shift for what the file can become, Ctrl for what to do with it (your own drop actions, ZIP, Copy to, Move to, Share, path, SHA-256). A file with nothing to become (a folder) gets the second on Shift too.
  • A GIF from a video is made of its first 30 seconds at most.
  • The dragged file stays in hand over the drop ring, captioned with what it becomes ("+ JPG"), as it is over Explorer. It used to vanish, and the pointer showed a "no entry" sign in the middle of the ring.

Fixed

  • A long video to WebM is no longer stopped after two hours: FFmpeg runs for as long as it keeps making progress, and one that stops is closed with a clear message.
  • A password-protected PowerPoint file fails at once with "password-protected" instead of waiting on a hidden password prompt.
  • "10 MB" / "25 MB" on a video whose length Windows cannot read (a browser's WebM, an MKV) asks FFmpeg, instead of calling a short clip too long.
  • "No location" on a damaged PNG no longer hangs.
  • Quitting while a conversion runs stops it and removes its half-written file.
  • Renaming the ring picked for "Your drop actions" keeps it picked.
  • radialdeck.com: a download or Store click is never lost to the counter failing; the stats need the owner's token.

RadialDeck 0.14.0

2026-10-19

Added

  • Drop ring (Pro): while dragging a file, press Shift and a ring of what it can become opens under the pointer; drop it on a slice. Images: smaller, JPG, PNG, one PDF. Video: smaller, MP4, MP3, GIF (with FFmpeg). Audio: MP3, M4A, WAV. PDF: pages as PNG or JPG, smaller and merge (with Ghostscript). Word, Excel and PowerPoint files to PDF when Office is installed. Dropping the file anywhere else still moves or copies it as before. The new file lands next to the original, which is never changed; a toast shows the sizes and opens it in Explorer.
  • The drop ring does much more than convert (Pro). Pictures: copy the text in them, a copy without location and EXIF (a JPG or PNG keeps every pixel), rotate, a 1080 px square, a Windows icon, copy to the clipboard. Video: a frame as JPG, without sound, and with FFmpeg under 10 or 25 MB (for e-mail or Discord), WebM and a better GIF. Excel to CSV and CSV to Excel. A ZIP is extracted beside itself. Any file or folder: ZIP, Copy to… and Move to… a folder you pick, Share (Windows' Share panel), copy the path, SHA-256. When there are more than eight, the last slice is "More": rest on it and the next page shows.
  • Your own drop actions (Pro): pick a ring in Settings › Options and its slices show in the drop ring too, run on the dropped files ({file}, {files}, $env:RD_FILE). Open in your editor, upload with your script, file into a folder.
  • "Convert the selected files" slice (Pro): the same ring for the files selected in Explorer, from your hotkey. In the gallery under Explorer.
  • Settings: a box to turn the drop ring off.
  • "What RadialDeck can do": every feature on one page, Free and Pro, each with how to use it and a button to where it lives. In the tray menu, in Settings › Options, at the end of the tutorial, and one click away from the "Pro is active" toast, so someone who just bought Pro sees what they bought.
  • Two more tips under the ring: the drop ring, and where the feature page is.
  • How the ring opens (Pro): Fade, Pop, Bloom, Spin or Ripple instead of Instant, with a Preview button in Appearance.
  • Apple style (preview), in Appearance: a translucent sidebar with coloured icons, a toolbar title, inset grouped settings, iOS switches and Apple blue in every RadialDeck window. It changes the open window at once; Save keeps it, Close puts the old look back.

Fixed

  • Word, Excel and PowerPoint files are converted with their macros switched off, a password-protected one fails at once instead of waiting for a hidden password prompt, and Office is closed again even when a conversion fails.
  • A failed or cancelled FFmpeg / Ghostscript run no longer leaves a half-written file behind, a "%" in a file name no longer confuses Ghostscript, and Ghostscript is found in its own folder (its installer does not add it to PATH).
  • Shift held from before a drag (selecting text, moving in Explorer, a straight line in a drawing app) no longer opens the drop ring; it opens when Shift is pressed during the drag.
  • Copy to / Move to a folder inside the folder itself is refused instead of copying it into itself.
  • An opening animation no longer starts with a flash of the ring at full size: Windows showed the ring's last picture before the new first frame, so it is now emptied before the ring hides.

Changed

  • Settings is one window with pages on the left: Rings, Gallery, Discover ("What RadialDeck can do", now inside the app), Appearance and Options, with your plan (Pro, trial or Free) at the foot. Every page has its title on top and Save / Close below. Appearance and Options are no longer pop-up panels: each setting is a row with its explanation under the name and its switch or list on the right, in groups.
  • Reopening Welcome with Pro active says so, instead of explaining the trial.
  • A fresher look in the Windows 11 style: on/off settings are switches, the ring size slider has a round thumb, "What RadialDeck can do" is grouped into Basics, Every day and Pro.
  • The licence window and the trial reminders list file conversion with the other Pro features.

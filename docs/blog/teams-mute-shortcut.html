Teams shortcut to mute and unmute (and hold-to-talk)

Ctrl+Shift+M mutes and unmutes you in a Microsoft Teams meeting, Ctrl+Space unmutes while you hold it, and Windows+Alt+K works from the taskbar. When each one fails, and how to mute from anywhere.

In a Microsoft Teams meeting on Windows, press Ctrl+Shift+M to mute or unmute your microphone. On a Mac it is Cmd+Shift+M.

Teams has two more ways that help: hold Ctrl+Space to unmute only while you hold it, and Win+Alt+K mutes from the Windows taskbar even when the meeting window is not in front.

The three shortcuts

Ctrl+Shift+M: toggle mute

Microsoft documents Ctrl+Shift+M as the mute and unmute shortcut during a meeting, in the desktop app and in Teams on the web. Press it once to mute, again to unmute.

Ctrl+Space: hold to talk

If you stay muted most of the meeting, hold Ctrl+Space to unmute for as long as the keys are down, say your sentence, and let go: you are muted again. On a Mac it is Option+Space.

It has to be switched on first: in Teams, go to Settings > Privacy and turn on Keyboard shortcut to unmute.

Windows+Alt+K: from the taskbar

Windows itself has a mute shortcut for calls, Win+Alt+K. Microsoft describes it as muting and unmuting the microphone in supported apps, and Teams is one of them. It is the one to use when another window is in front and you need to mute quickly.

Why the mute shortcut does nothing

  • The cursor is in the chat box. Microsoft notes that shortcuts, including the hold-to-talk one, do not work inside the chat box. Click outside it first.
  • Something is shared on the meeting stage. The same note says shortcuts do not work while apps such as PowerPoint Live or Whiteboard are shared in the meeting stage.
  • Teams is not the active window. Ctrl+Shift+M goes to whatever window is in front. If you are in a browser or a document, it does something there instead. Use Win+Alt+K in that case.
  • Hold-to-talk is off. Ctrl+Space only works after you turn on Keyboard shortcut to unmute under Settings > Privacy.
  • Another program has the keys. Some tools register Ctrl+Shift+M or Ctrl+Space for themselves; Ctrl+Space is also used by some keyboard layouts and input methods to switch input. If one never works, try the other route.

Quick reference

  • Mute / unmute: Ctrl+Shift+M (Mac Cmd+Shift+M)

VLC shortcut for playback speed: faster, slower and back to normal

In VLC, + and - change the playback speed, ] and [ change it in fine steps, and = returns to normal. Why + sometimes does nothing, and how to set your own keys.

In VLC, press + to play faster and - to play slower. Press = to go back to normal speed.

For finer steps, use ] (a little faster) and [ (a little slower). These are the default hotkeys listed on the VideoLAN wiki.

The speed keys

  • Faster: +
  • Slower: -
  • Normal rate: =
  • Faster (fine): ]
  • Slower (fine): [

The fine steps are useful for lectures and podcasts, where a little faster is comfortable and a lot faster is not. VLC shows the current rate on the video for a moment after each press.

There is also a menu for it: Playback > Speed, with Faster, Slower and Normal Speed.

Why the speed keys do nothing

  • The plus key on the main keyboard. On most keyboards the + above the letters is really Shift+=, and since = on its own means "normal rate", the plus may not do what you expect. The + on the numeric keypad is a plus sign without Shift. If you have no keypad, use ] instead, or set your own key (below).
  • A different keyboard layout. On many non-English layouts, [, ] and = are on other keys or need AltGr. Set keys you can reach in the hotkey settings.
  • VLC is not the active window. The hotkeys go to the window in front. Click the video once.
  • A text field has focus. In the playlist search or a dialog, keys type there instead.

Set your own speed keys

VLC lets you change every hotkey:

  1. Open Tools > Preferences.
  2. Select the Hotkeys panel.
  3. Find Faster, Slower, Faster (fine), Slower (fine) and Normal rate in the list.
  4. Double-click an action, press the key you want, and confirm.
  5. Select Save.

Pick keys that work on your layout without AltGr, for example two letters that VLC does not already use. The list shows which keys are taken.

Quick reference

  • Faster / slower: + / - (numeric keypad plus is the reliable one)
  • Fine steps: ] / [
  • Normal speed: =
  • Menu: Playback > Speed
  • Your own keys: Tools > Preferences > Hotkeys

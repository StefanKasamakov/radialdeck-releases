Terms of use

Last updated 2 October 2026. These are the terms under which you may use RadialDeck. They are short on purpose.

Free

You may install and use the free version on as many computers as you like, at home or at work, for as long as you like.

Pro

  • A Pro licence is for one person, on the computers that person uses. It is not tied to a machine; move it whenever you want.
  • It is a yearly subscription (24 € a year, VAT included) that renews automatically until you cancel, which you can do at any time from your Lemon Squeezy receipt or by writing to us.
  • While it is paid, you get every update. If a renewal payment is late, Pro keeps working for 14 more days.
  • Your key works offline, but a subscription has to be confirmed online now and then: RadialDeck checks with radialdeck.com once a day when it can, and if it has not been able to for 60 days, Pro pauses until the computer is online again. Nothing is lost while it waits.
  • If you cancel, Pro turns off when the paid period ends, whichever version you have installed; RadialDeck keeps working as the free version and your rings stay in your config. Subscribe again and Pro comes back with them.
  • Please do not share your key publicly. A key that is published may be blocked for future versions.

Team

A Team licence costs 10 € per seat per year, with a minimum of 5 seats. It covers the number of people (seats) you bought, for one year, including updates during that year. Without a renewal, Pro features turn off for those seats when the year ends, as with Pro; the free version keeps working.

Payment and refunds

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller of record and takes care of payment, invoices and VAT. You can get a full refund within 30 days, no questions asked; see Refunds.

What RadialDeck does on your computer

RadialDeck runs the actions you configure: it starts programs, sends keystrokes, pastes text and runs commands you wrote or picked from the gallery. You are responsible for what your own actions do. Commands from the gallery are shown to you before you add them.

No warranty

RadialDeck is provided as it is. We work to keep it reliable, but we cannot promise it will be free of errors or suit every purpose. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for indirect damage or lost data caused by using it. Nothing here limits the rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country.

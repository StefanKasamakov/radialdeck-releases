Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026.

What RadialDeck collects

Nothing about you. The application has no telemetry, no crash reporting, no analytics and no accounts. It never contacts our servers.

The only network request it makes is an optional update check: every few hours it asks GitHub Releases (api.github.com) for the latest version number and, if you click "install", downloads the installer from GitHub. The request carries no identifier other than the application name and version. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that request. You can turn the check off in Settings ("Check for updates"); the app then makes no network requests at all.

What stays on your computer

  • %APPDATA%\RadialDeck\config.json: your rings and actions. Yours to edit, export or delete.
  • %APPDATA%\RadialDeck\license.json: your licence key and the date of your first run (for the trial). Verified locally.
  • %APPDATA%\RadialDeck\debug.log: lifecycle events and errors. It never contains keystrokes or clipboard contents.

The clipboard history ring

One optional slice type opens a ring of the last few things you copied, so you can paste an earlier one. It is off unless you add that slice to a ring yourself, and only then does RadialDeck ask Windows to tell it when the clipboard changes.

Those entries live in the application's memory and nowhere else. They are never written to config.json, never written to the log, never sent anywhere, and they are gone the moment RadialDeck closes or you remove the slice. What you copy while the feature is off is not seen at all.

What your recipes may do

A recipe is an action you chose: opening a URL, running a command, pasting text. Those actions behave like anything else you run on your PC; for example "Ask ChatGPT about the copied text" opens chatgpt.com with that text in your browser. RadialDeck itself does not transmit it.

Purchases

Pro and Team licences are sold through our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy), which processes your payment details and e-mail under its own privacy policy. We receive your name and e-mail to issue the licence key and to answer support requests. We do not sell or share them.

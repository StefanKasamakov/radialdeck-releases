Your Shift key has a hidden menu

Every app gets its own ring

Hold right-click in Excel and you get Excel's ring; in Chrome, Chrome's; in AutoCAD, AutoCAD's. Same gesture, the right ring. Rings per app are Pro. 21 s

Every open window, by name

Hold right-click, rest on Windows and every open window shows up by name. Flick to the one you want and it comes to the front. 21 s

PDF too big to send? Shrink it

Select the PDF in Explorer, hold right-click, flick to Shrink PDF: a smaller copy lands next to it (27.1 MB became 2.6 MB in our test). Uses the free Ghostscript; your original stays. 21 s

Stop uploading files to converter sites

Drag the photo, tap Shift, drop it on JPG: done on your PC in seconds, and the file never leaves it. HEIC needs Microsoft's free HEIF extension. Pro. 19 s

Duplicates in Excel? One flick

Hold right-click in Excel, flick to Dedupe and press OK: Excel's own Remove Duplicates, every name exactly once. 19 s

Copy text you can't select

Hold right-click, flick to Screen text, drag over a paused video, a slide or a scan. Windows' own OCR reads it; nothing is uploaded. 21 s

AutoCAD: Zoom extents in one flick

Hold right-click in AutoCAD and flick to Zoom extents: it types the command, Enter included. Your top eight commands on one ring. Macros are Pro. 20 s

Your scroll wheel is a button

Hold the middle button and a ring opens: reopen a closed tab, keep a video on top, mute, snip. Any mouse button can open it. 15 s

Your clipboard can do more than paste
